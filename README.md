# Introduction
The Love Laundry Report project was created to analyze operational and revenue performance for a growing laundry business offering five core services: dry cleaning, laundry, mending, stain removal, and ironing. With a dataset consisting of 100 customer orders across multiple transaction modes (online, cash, and credit card), the project aims to uncover critical business insights using Power BI.

Through interactive visualizations, this report explores key business metrics such as total income, top-paying customers, service-specific earnings, and payment behavior. It also analyzes trends in income over time and identifies areas where the business can optimize for growth. By turning raw transactional data into an easy-to-read dashboard, the project highlights how data analytics can drive informed decision-making in small service-based enterprises.

# Project Aim
To analyze customer orders and revenue data from a laundry business to uncover insights that improve business performance, optimize service delivery, and enhance customer experience. The project leverages Power BI to visualize key metrics such as income by service type, customer payment trends, and monthly revenue patterns.

# Project Description
This project focuses on building a comprehensive Power BI dashboard for a laundry service business. The business offers five core services: Dry Cleaning, Laundry, Mending, Stain Removal, and Ironing. The dataset includes transaction information such as payment type, customer names, income generated, machine usage, and payment status. By creating visual insights from the data, the project demonstrates how data visualization can guide smarter operational and strategic decisions in small businesses.

4. Data Acquisition & Preparation
The dataset was provided in Excel format and contained records of 100 laundry service transactions. The key columns included:

Customer Name

Services Rendered

Payment Status

Income Amount

Payment Mode (Online, Cash, Credit Card)

Order Count

Machine Count

Date of Transaction

The dataset was imported into Power BI using the Get Data option.

# Data Cleaning & Transformation
Power BI’s Power Query Editor was used to:

Format data types (e.g., income as currency, date as datetime).

Create calculated columns and measures such as Total Income, Income by Service, and Monthly Income Trend.

Standardize service names for consistent categorization.

Split dates where necessary to extract month names for trend analysis.

Derive new metrics like Total Number of Machines and Top Paying Customers.

# Data Analysis & Visualization
Visualizations were carefully selected to convey meaningful insights:

KPI Cards: Displayed total customers, total income, number of orders, machines used, and service types offered.

Bar Charts: Highlighted the top 10 paying customers and income by service type.

Line Chart: Tracked income trends across months.

Column Chart: Showed paid vs unpaid order count.

Slicers: Allowed dynamic filtering by service type.

Payment Type Cards: Summarized transaction income by online, cash, and credit card payments.

All visualizations were styled for clarity, interactivity, and impact.

# Insights & Interpretation

- Total income generated was ₦426K, across 100 customers and 100 orders.

- The top-paying customer contributed ₦31K.

- Dry Cleaning was the highest revenue-generating service with ₦114K.

- The majority of income came from cash transactions (₦191K), followed by online (₦125K) and credit card (₦111K).

- October had the highest monthly income (₦152K), while September saw the lowest (₦35K).

- 61% of orders were paid, while 39% remained unpaid, indicating a need for improved payment collection or tracking.

# Recommendations
Based on the dashboard analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

- Promote Top Services: Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue services like Dry Cleaning and Laundry to further boost income.

- Customer Segmentation: Create loyalty programs for top-paying customers to encourage repeat patronage.

- Address Unpaid Orders: Investigate reasons for unpaid transactions and implement reminders or discounts for early payment.

- Revenue Drop Investigation: The sharp income decline in September should be reviewed. Possible causes might include seasonality, machine downtime, or customer churn.

- Diversify Services: Consider expanding underperforming services like Ironing through bundled offers or promotions.

By implementing these recommendations, the organization can optimize its sales strategies, improve inventory management, enhance customer experience, and maximize revenue across different periods and locations.
